# M-Unite

The purpose of the M-Unite system is to provide a centralized, web-based platform that enhances communication and transparency between the Makana Municipality and Makhanda residents.
M-Unite will allow residents to report issues related to water, sanitation, electricity, and roads, track the progress of their reports, and receive timely updates.

The system also supports ward councilors, municipal officers, and system administrators, providing role-based access and tools to monitor, manage, and communicate service delivery effectively. Ultimately, the platform aims to foster accountability, community engagement, and transparency in municipal service delivery
