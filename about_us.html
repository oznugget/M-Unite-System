About M-Unite

Our Goals & Mission

M-Unite is dedicated to bridging the communication gap between the residents of Makhanda and the local municipality. Our primary objective is to empower citizens with a direct, transparent platform to report municipal issues such as water leaks, road defects, and electrical faults while tracking resolution progress in real time.

We aim to foster active civic participation, ensure timely public notices, and promote community resilience through open data and collaborative problem-solving.
